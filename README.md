### Project Description:

Our project is meant to be a simplified model of how elections in the Philippines work under different situations and circumstances throughout history. It could help more people understand and be more aware of democracy and the government system in the Philippines. Although our program will be accessible to a wide demographic, it would be better to have it aimed to students studying this topic so that they could have a hands-on program for it along with people who just want to find out more.

First of all, there needs to be an election setting in the first place. Here, we can see the year which the election is held in, the number of days left before official polls are released, the turns per day, the candidates, and the events that could happen. A main method that could be found is showing the official polls which should also determine if the player has won. Also, there are events which is made up of a question and choices to answer, in which it could affect how much some regions could support the candidate more. Of course, there will also be the actual candidates, which have the attributes of a name, party, and if whether they are the player. Lastly, the region also contains its own name, number of voters, and support for the candidate which is private. Events and regions are directly connected to the election although candidates can be reused for another election season.

It is important to note that although our main concept is bound to be related to what our description shows, almost everything else will be subject to change. :)
